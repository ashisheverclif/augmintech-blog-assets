Architectural drafting is the production of accurate, standardised drawings that tell a builder exactly what to construct: site plans, floor plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules. In India, IS 962 sets the drafting conventions, such as A-series sheets, plans at 1:50 or 1:100 and details at 1:10 or 1:20. Most drafting today happens in AutoCAD, but larger projects are moving to BIM, where drawings come from a model in software such as Revit. This guide covers every drawing type, the standards and skills employers test, what draftsmen earn, and a practical path from CAD to BIM.

TL;DR

  • Drafting turns design into buildable instructions: plans, sections, elevations, details and schedules, all to scale and standard.
  • IS 962 is India's drafting standard: A0–A4 sheets, title block bottom right, first-angle projection, thick lines at least twice thin ones.
  • Employers test four things: reading drawings, construction knowledge, clean CAD technique, and accuracy.
  • Draftsmen average about ₹4.6 lakh a year on PayScale, with freshers around ₹1.8 lakh.
  • BIM is the next step: from drawing lines to modelling buildings, which opens BIM modeller, coordinator and manager roles.

What Architectural Drafting Is

An architect designs a building; drafting documents it so that structural engineers, services consultants, authorities and contractors can all work from the same information. A draftsman's drawings must be accurate to the millimetre, complete, consistent between sheets and readable by people who were not in the design meetings.

Drafting is done by architectural draftsmen (often diploma holders), CAD technicians, junior architects and, increasingly, BIM modellers. The title changes, but the core skill is the same: translating a design into clear, correct, standard drawings.

Most offices build these rules into a CAD template: layers, lineweights, text styles, dimension styles and a title block. Using the office template consistently is often what separates a reliable draftsman from a fast but messy one.

The Drafting Workflow, Step by Step

  1. Set up from the templateStart from the office template with the right layers, styles, sheet sizes and title block.
  2. Draw the grid and structureGrid lines and columns from the structural drawings fix everything else.
  3. Draw walls, openings and levelsFloor by floor, keeping wall thicknesses and levels consistent.
  4. Derive sections and elevationsProject them from the plans, checking heights against the section.
  5. Detail and annotateDimensions, room names, levels, tags, notes and hatches, then details at larger scale.
  6. Set up layouts and sheetsViewports at the right scales, sheet numbers and a drawing list.
  7. Check, then issueSelf-check against the checklist, get sign-off, plot to PDF, record the revision.
  8. Revise with controlEvery change gets a revision cloud, letter or number, date and description.

10 Drafting Mistakes That Cost Credibility

  1. Dimensions that do not add up to the overall dimension.
  2. Plans and sections that disagree on heights or openings.
  3. Everything on one layer or at one lineweight, so the drawing is unreadable when printed.
  4. Exploded blocks and loose lines that make later changes slow.
  5. Text and dimensions at the wrong scale, tiny or huge on the sheet.
  6. Missing north point, scale or level references.
  7. Uncontrolled revisions: changes made without clouds or revision numbers.
  8. Copying old details that do not match this building.
  9. Ignoring the structural drawings, so beams clash with windows or ceilings.
  10. Not checking before issue, leaving the architect to find errors.

The Drafting Test in Interviews

Most offices test draftsmen with a practical task, usually 60 to 120 minutes. Common formats:

Redraw from a sketchA hand sketch of a small house plan to draft in AutoCAD with walls, openings, dimensions and room names.Tests speed, layers, accuracy
Derive a section or elevationA floor plan with levels, from which you draw a section through the staircase or an elevation.Tests construction understanding
Find the errorsA drawing with deliberate mistakes: wrong dimensions, missing tags, plan and section that disagree.Tests checking discipline

To prepare, practise drafting a two-bedroom plan from a sketch in under an hour, with clean layers and correct dimension styles, and review how a staircase and a toilet are detailed. In the test, organise layers first, dimension as you go, and leave five minutes to check before you save.

Does Hand Drafting Still Matter?

Hand drafting is rarely used for final drawings, but freehand sketching still matters every day: to understand an architect's idea, to explain a detail to a site supervisor, or to work out a junction before drawing it in CAD. Draftsmen who can sketch a quick section by hand usually understand construction better, and it shows in their CAD work.

The Path From CAD to BIM

BIM (building information modelling) changes the job from drawing lines to building a model. In Revit, you place walls, floors, doors and rooms as objects with data; plans, sections, elevations and schedules are views of that model and update together. For a draftsman, three things change:

In CAD drafting

  • You draw each view separately
  • Consistency between sheets depends on you
  • Schedules are typed tables
  • Coordination happens by overlaying drawings

In BIM

  • You model once; views are generated
  • The model keeps sheets consistent
  • Schedules read from the model
  • Coordination happens in combined 3D models with clash checks

What carries over: your understanding of construction, standards, sheet setup and checking. Good draftsmen often become good BIM modellers because they know what the drawings must show. What is new: thinking in levels, element types and families, controlling views with templates, and working in a shared model.

A 90-day plan for draftsmen moving to Revit

  1. Days 1–30: Model basicsLevels, grids, walls, floors, roofs, doors, windows and stairs; rebuild a project you have already drafted.
  2. Days 31–60: DocumentationViews and view templates, dimensions, tags, rooms, schedules, sheets and title blocks; produce the full drawing set from your model.
  3. Days 61–90: Teamwork and coordinationFamilies, linking DWG and consultant models, worksharing, and exporting DWG and IFC.

Learning Revit alone is possible, but the workflow and standards are hard to pick up from button tutorials. A structured route, such as Augmintech's PG Program in BIM Architecture, is one option to compare; ask any provider to show sample student drawing sets. Students can use Revit free for learning through Autodesk's education plan; professionals need a commercial licence for paid work.

Options for Diploma Holders

  • Work as a draftsman first: a diploma in architecture or civil engineering plus strong AutoCAD skills is the classic entry into architects' and builders' offices.
  • Add BIM: Revit skills move you towards BIM modelling roles on larger projects and in BIM service firms.
  • Upgrade to B.Arch: 10+3 diploma holders with Mathematics and at least 45% aggregate are listed as eligible for NATA (2027 cycle), the entrance for B.Arch; confirm on the official NATA site.
  • Specialise: submission drawings, interior working drawings, or quantity estimation all build on drafting skills.

Worked Revision: Move a Door Without Breaking the Set

Suppose a client asks to shift an internal door 300 mm. Start from the latest approved source file and confirm the wall, opening and design instruction. Change the plan geometry, then review the corresponding elevation or section, dimensions, door tag, schedule and any nearby joinery or ceiling references.

In AutoCAD, review affected drawings and references deliberately. In Revit, inspect the hosted door, constraints, relevant views and schedule filters. Model relationships help coordinate the change, but they do not rewrite every manual note or prove that clearances remain acceptable.

  1. Geometry: check opening size, swing, wall junction and furniture conflicts.
  2. Information: reconcile tags, dimensions, schedule entries and detail references.
  3. Presentation: plot the intended paper size and inspect text, lineweights, hatches and viewport scale.
  4. Issue control: record the revision, follow the agreed approval process and keep the superseded issue identifiable.

Common Failures and the First Check

If a DWG arrives at the wrong size, confirm units using a known dimension before scaling anything. If a PDF has unreadable text, inspect annotation scale and plotting settings. If a Revit element disappears in one view, review category visibility, phase, view range and the view template before drawing a replacement with detail lines.

A useful practice submission includes the source file, the issued PDF and a short revision log. Explain one error you found and how you corrected it. This shows drafting judgement as well as command knowledge.

Drafting and BIM Jobs in the Gulf

Gulf construction employs many Indian draftsmen and BIM modellers, especially on large commercial, hospitality and infrastructure projects. These projects increasingly require BIM: since 1 January 2024, Dubai has required BIM model submissions in IFC format for building permits on larger buildings, as summarised on Autodesk's Dubai BIM mandate page. Job adverts there often ask for both AutoCAD and Revit, plus experience with shop drawings and coordination.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is architectural drafting?
It is the production of accurate, standardised drawings, such as plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules, that communicate a building design to engineers, authorities and builders.
What scales are used in architectural drawings?
IS 962 recommends 1:500 or 1:1000 for site plans, 1:50 or 1:100 for plans and elevations, and 1:10 or 1:20 for details.
Which software is used for architectural drafting?
AutoCAD is the most common for 2D drafting. Revit is used for BIM, where drawings are generated from a 3D model. Many offices use both.
How much does an architectural draftsman earn in India?
PayScale reports an average base pay of about ₹4.58 lakh a year (27 profiles, June 2026), with entry-level total pay around ₹1.83 lakh.
Can a draftsman become a BIM modeller?
Yes, and it is one of the most natural moves. Drafting knowledge carries over; you add Revit modelling, view control, schedules and coordination.
Can a diploma holder become an architect?
Yes, by completing a B.Arch. Diploma holders with Mathematics can be eligible for NATA, the B.Arch entrance; check the current year's rules.
Is drafting being replaced by BIM?
Drafting is changing rather than disappearing. On larger projects, drawings increasingly come from BIM models, but someone still needs to set up, check and complete those drawings to standard.
