BOQBill of Quantities

BOQ stands for Bill of Quantities. It is a document that lists every item of work in a project with its description, unit and quantity, so contractors can price the same scope at tender and the client can control cost through construction.

Below you will find a sample MEP BOQ you can price yourself, a BOQ vs BOM comparison, the steps to prepare a BOQ, and how to pull quantities out of Revit.

TL;DR

  • BOQ full form is Bill of Quantities: a list of work items with descriptions, units and firm quantities.
  • A quantity surveyor or cost consultant usually prepares it once the design and specifications are complete.
  • Contractors price the BOQ at tender, which gives every bidder the same basis and makes bids comparable.
  • An MEP BOQ needs six columns: item number, description, unit, quantity, rate and amount.
  • A BOQ is for tendering and cost control; a BOM is a component list for procurement and production.
  • Quantities follow a named method of measurement, such as IS 1200 in India or the method named in a Gulf tender.

What Is a Bill of Quantities (BOQ)?

A bill of quantities is, in the RICS NRM2 definition quoted by RIB Software, "a list of items that gives detailed identifying descriptions and firm quantities of the work comprising a contract" (RIB Software). It is "a document prepared by a quantity surveyor or cost consultant" (Jones Melling), and is usually issued to bidding contractors as part of the tender documents once design and specifications are complete.

A BOQ in construction does more than one job across the project:

TenderingAll bidders price the same items, so bids can be compared fairly.
PricingThe priced BOQ becomes the basis of the contract sum.
Cost controlBudgets, forecasts and interim payments are tracked against it.
VariationsIts rates are used to value changes and the final account.

Typical sections include preliminaries, measured work, provisional sums, dayworks and a summary (RIB Software). For MEP, the measured work is usually split by trade: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, fire fighting and ELV.

6 Columns Every MEP BOQ Needs

Col 1Item No.A unique reference (1.1, 1.2) used in queries, payments and variations.
Col 2DescriptionWhat, size, rating, and what is included: supply, install, test, commission.
Col 3Unitm², Rm, No., Set or kg, as the method of measurement sets.
Col 4QuantityThe measured quantity from the drawings.
Col 5RateThe bidder's price per unit, filled in at tender.
Col 6AmountQuantity × rate, summed into subtotals and the total.

BOQ vs BOM

The two are often confused because both list quantities. They serve different people at different stages.

BOQ (Bill of Quantities)
BOM (Bill of Materials)
Purpose
Tendering, pricing and cost control of construction work
Planning, procurement and production of a product or assembly
Prepared by
Quantity surveyor or cost consultant
Engineering, production or procurement teams
Stage
Pre-construction, issued with the tender
Detailed design, before procurement and production
Pricing

Detail

Components down to part numbers and specifications

Based on Kreo and Netstock; "prepared by" for the BOQ from Jones Melling. On MEP projects, contractors often prepare BOMs from the approved BOQ and shop drawings to order material.

How to Prepare a BOQ

  1. Read drawings and specsCollect the latest drawings, specifications, schedules and the tender's method of measurement.
  2. Break into tradesSplit into HVAC, plumbing, electrical, fire and ELV, then into systems and floors.
  3. Take off quantitiesMeasure lengths, areas and counts from drawings or the model, following the measurement rules.
  4. Describe each itemState material, size, rating and inclusions so every bidder prices the same thing.
  5. PriceContractors add rates; the consultant prepares a priced estimate to check bids against.
  6. ReviewCross-check totals against drawings, remove duplicates, and confirm every specified item is billed.
Method of measurement. In India, IS 1200 sets measurement rules for building and civil works, part by part. Part 19 covers water supply, plumbing and drains, and Part 16 covers laying of water and sewer lines (BIS IS 1200 parts list). IS 1200 has no part for electrical or HVAC work, so for those trades the measurement rules are written into the tender specification. On Gulf projects, the tender documents name the method of measurement to follow.

Quantity Takeoff from Revit Schedules

On a BIM project, most MEP quantities can come straight out of the model. A Revit schedule is a live table of model elements, so when the model changes, the quantities change with it.

Step 1Create schedulesDuct, pipe, fitting, air terminal, plumbing fixture and lighting fixture schedules.
Step 2Pick fieldsSystem type, size, length and count; add filters by level or system.
Step 3Group and totalSort by size, untick "Itemize every instance", and show totals.
Step 4Export to ExcelExport the schedule, open it in Excel, and map lines to BOQ items.
File tab › Export › Reports › Schedule › choose delimiter › Save

Revit saves the schedule "as delimited text, a format that can be opened in spreadsheet programs, such as Microsoft Excel" (Autodesk Revit Help). In Excel, convert schedule data into BOQ units:

Revit scheduleFieldsBOQ item and unit
Duct scheduleWidth, height, lengthDuct sheet area in m²: 2 × (W + H) × length
Pipe scheduleSystem type, size, lengthPipe by size and system in Rm
Air terminal scheduleFamily and type, countDiffusers and grilles by type in No.
Plumbing fixture scheduleFamily and type, countSanitary fixtures in No.
Lighting fixture scheduleFamily and type, countLight fittings in No.

A model only gives what has been modelled. Hangers, sealant, testing and small fittings often still need allowances or manual takeoff. Linking quantities like this to cost is what 5D BIM means; our BIM scheduling page covers it in more depth.

BOQs on Gulf Projects

Method named in the tenderThe BOQ preamble states which method of measurement applies; quantities and descriptions must follow it.
POMI is widely knownA survey of Middle East quantity surveyors found POMI had "the most recognition", followed by CESMM; NRM was "still not well recognized" (Magdy and Ezeldin, 2016).
BOQ status depends on contractIn re-measurement or unit-price contracts the BOQ ranks high among contract documents; in lump-sum contracts it is mainly used to value variations.

Survey source: Magdy, A. and Ezeldin, S. (2016), ISEC Press, a questionnaire of 140 quantity surveying professionals with 45 replies. Always read the tender's own preamble and BOQ format before pricing or preparing quantities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the full form of BOQ?
BOQ stands for Bill of Quantities.
What is a BOQ in construction?
A document listing every item of work with its description, unit and quantity, issued with the tender so contractors can price the same scope. The priced BOQ is then used for the contract sum, interim payments and valuing variations.
What is the difference between a BOQ and a BOM?
A BOQ lists priced work items for tendering and cost control in construction. A BOM lists the components and materials needed to make or procure something, usually without rates, for production and purchasing.
Who prepares the BOQ?
Usually a quantity surveyor or cost consultant on behalf of the client, once the design and specifications are complete. Contractors then fill in the rates when they bid.
How do you take off quantities from Revit?
Create schedules for ducts, pipes, fittings and fixtures, add fields such as size, length and count, group and total them, then export via File › Export › Reports › Schedule as delimited text and open the file in Excel to map quantities to BOQ items.
