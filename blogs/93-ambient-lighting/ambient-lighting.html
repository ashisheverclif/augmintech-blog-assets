Ambient lighting is the general light that fills a room evenly, so you can see and move around safely. Good rooms layer it with task lighting for activities and accent lighting for focal points. Further down, you can switch the same living room between the three layers and see the difference for yourself.

TL;DR

  • Ambient lighting sets the base level with coves, downlights, panels and chandeliers spread evenly across the room.
  • Task lighting puts brighter light where you work, such as 300 to 500 lux for reading, against 100 to 200 lux for a living room in general.
  • Accent lighting is about contrast: a feature needs at least three times the surrounding brightness to stand out.
  • Beam angle separates the layers, from wide 60° downlights for ambient light to narrow 24° spots for accents.
  • A lighting plan shows each fixture, its switching and a fixture schedule on the reflected ceiling plan.
  • Gulf villas and majlis rooms favour warm 2,700 to 3,000 K layered light, with coves and chandeliers.

Ambient Lighting

Ambient lighting replaces daylight after dark. Its job is to light the room evenly, without dark corners or hard shadows, so the space reads clearly at a glance. Crompton's guide calls it simply "the general illumination of a space".

Cove lightingLED strip hidden in a false-ceiling cove, bouncing light off the ceiling. The softest ambient light, with no visible source.
Recessed downlightsWide-beam fittings in a regular grid. Spacing them evenly is what makes the light even.
LED panelsFlat 600 × 600 mm or round panels, common in offices and kitchens with grid ceilings.
Chandeliers and pendantsDecorative ambient sources, usually over a dining table or at the centre of a double-height room.

Even light comes from layout, not wattage: a regular grid of fittings, beams that overlap at working height, and light bounced off pale ceilings and walls. A typical ambient downlight has a wide beam. For example, SAL's Coolum Plus is:

Task Lighting

Task lighting is brighter, more focused light where a specific job happens. It is there because ambient light alone is not enough for detail work. India's National Building Code (NBC 2016, Part 8) recommends 100 to 200 lux in a living room in general, but 300 to 500 lux for reading, provided locally, and 300 to 500 lux on a kitchen counter.

Under-cabinet lightsLED strips or profiles under wall cabinets, lighting the counter with no head shadow.
Reading lightsFloor lamps, bedside lamps or wall-mounted swing arms aimed over the shoulder.
Desk lampsAdjustable lamps that light the keyboard or paper, placed opposite the writing hand.
Vanity lightsLights on both sides of a mirror, at face height, so the face is lit without shadows under the eyes.

Accent Lighting

Accent lighting draws the eye to something: a painting, a textured stone wall, a niche, a plant. It works through contrast. If the accent is barely brighter than the room, nobody notices it. Lighting Design Lab's guidance gives these ratios of accent to ambient light:

2:1Perceptible but "flat"
3:1Minimum to read as an accent
5:1Distinct focal area
10:1Strong focal accent
15:1Dramatic focal accent
SpotlightsAdjustable recessed or track spots with narrow beams, aimed at a single object.
Wall washersAsymmetric fittings that light a whole wall evenly, ideal for stone, cladding or a feature colour.
Picture lightsSmall fittings mounted above a frame, lighting the art from close range.
Profile lightsLED strip in an aluminium channel with a diffuser, set into niches, shelves and ceiling lines.

Accent fittings use narrow beams to put light only where it is wanted. Integral's AccentPro downlight, for example, is:

A lighting plan is drawn on the reflected ceiling plan (RCP). It shows every fitting as a symbol, the switching that groups fittings into circuits, and a schedule that defines each fitting type. Here is a sample for a 6.0 × 4.5 m living room:

To draw it in AutoCAD:

  1. Start from the RCP: walls, ceiling levels and cove lines on their own layers, such as A-CLNG.
  2. Insert fixture blocks on a lighting layer such as E-LITE, one block per fitting type, tagged A, B, C.
  3. Draw switching as curved dashed lines (arcs) from each switch to the fittings it controls, on E-LITE-SWCH.
  4. Add the switch plate at the door, labelling switches (S1, S2…) and dimmers (D1).
  5. Attach the fixture schedule and a legend, so every tag points to a wattage, beam angle, colour temperature and quantity.

Lighting in Gulf villas and majlis rooms

  • Warm colour temperature. Algedra's Abu Dhabi majlis guide (November 2025) recommends about 2,700 to 3,000 K for a welcoming feel.
  • Layered light. The same guide says layered, warm lighting is widely used in Abu Dhabi villas, with coves, wall sconces and floor lamps as secondary layers.
  • Chandeliers as the centrepiece. Large crystal chandeliers give the room presence and scatter reflections across marble floors.
  • Put coves on dimmers. Majlis rooms host everything from formal visits to late evenings, so wire cove and chandelier circuits for dimming and scenes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ambient lighting?
Ambient lighting is the general light that fills a room evenly so you can see and move around safely. It usually comes from cove lights, recessed downlights, LED panels or chandeliers.
What is the difference between ambient and accent lighting?
Ambient lighting lights the whole room evenly with wide beams. Accent lighting uses narrow beams to make one object, such as a painting or textured wall, at least three times brighter than its surroundings so it stands out.
What are examples of task lighting?
Under-cabinet lights over a kitchen counter, reading lamps beside a sofa or bed, desk lamps, and vanity lights on each side of a bathroom mirror.
What are the types of lighting in interior design?
The three main types, or layers, are ambient, task and accent lighting. Some guides add decorative lighting (chandeliers and pendants chosen for their looks) and natural daylight as further types.
How do you draw a lighting plan?
Start from the reflected ceiling plan, insert a block for each fitting type, draw switching lines from each switch to the fittings it controls, then add a legend and a fixture schedule listing wattage, beam angle, colour temperature and quantity.
