Interior designers in India earn about ₹3.3 lakh a year in base pay on average, from about ₹2.5 lakh for freshers to around ₹9.8 lakh for the top 10% (PayScale, updated October 2026). In Dubai, GulfTalent puts the average at AED 6,000 a month, and a design recruiter's 2026 guide puts mid-level packages at AED 13,000 to 23,000. If you are a student or an engineer weighing a move into interior design, here is what the numbers really say, and how to get your first job.

TL;DR

  • Freshers in India start at about ₹2.5 lakh a year on average, according to PayScale's October 2026 data.
  • Pay roughly doubles by the mid-career stage, to about ₹5 to 6 lakh a year for 5 to 9 years' experience.
  • Mumbai pays the most on Indeed, at about ₹47,700 a month, against a national average of about ₹23,600.
  • Gulf figures vary widely by source: AED 6,000 a month on average on GulfTalent, but AED 13,000 to 40,000 packages for mid and senior roles in a recruiter's guide.
  • AutoCAD, SketchUp, V-Ray, 3ds Max and Photoshop are the core software set employers expect.
  • A focused portfolio of 4 to 6 projects, with plans, renders and process, matters more than the degree name.

How to Become an Interior Designer: Step by Step

  1. Pick an education route.A bachelor's degree in interior design (B.Des or B.Sc), a shorter diploma, or a degree in architecture or civil engineering. Studios hire from all of these; what they test is your drawings and portfolio.
  2. Learn the core software.AutoCAD first, because every project needs drawings, then SketchUp for 3D and V-Ray or 3ds Max for renders, and Photoshop for presentation.
  3. Build real projects.Redesign your own flat, a relative's shop or a café nearby. Measure the space, draw it, design it, render it and price it.
  4. Do an internship.Three to six months in a design studio, a fit-out contractor or a modular kitchen firm teaches you site work, vendors and client meetings that no course covers.
  5. Make a portfolio.Four to six projects with plans, renders and process, plus a one-page PDF version (see below).
  6. Land your first job.Apply as a junior designer, design associate, 3D visualiser or site designer. Your first role is about learning execution; specialise after two or three years.

Switching from civil engineering or architecture

  • You already have the hard part: reading and producing drawings, understanding structure and services, and site experience.
  • Fill the gaps: design principles, materials and finishes, lighting, and rendering in SketchUp, V-Ray or 3ds Max.
  • Use your edge: false ceilings, lighting, kitchens and wet areas all depend on structure and MEP coordination. Engineers who can design and coordinate are valuable to fit-out firms.
  • Common entry points: fit-out site engineer, design coordinator, or junior designer in a design-and-build company, then move into design leadership.

What a Good Interior Design Portfolio Looks Like

A portfolio gets you the interview; the degree rarely does. Keep it short and show how you think, not just how well you render.

  • 4 to 6 projects, strongest and most relevant first.
  • A mix of types: a flat, a villa or large home, and one commercial space.
  • For each project: the brief, a measured plan, concept and mood board, renders, and working drawings.
  • Process sheets showing options you rejected and why.
  • Real details: a ceiling plan, a kitchen elevation or a joinery section.
  • A one-page PDF under 5 MB, plus a link to the full version online.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the salary of an interior designer in India?
PayScale puts the average base salary at about ₹3.3 lakh a year (updated October 2026), with most designers between about ₹1.5 lakh and ₹9.8 lakh. Indeed's average is ₹23,598 a month, about ₹2.8 lakh a year (June 2026).
What is the salary of a fresher interior designer?
About ₹2.5 lakh a year on average for designers with under one year's experience, according to PayScale (132 salaries, October 2026). Big-city studios and designers with strong software skills can start higher.
How much do interior designers earn in Dubai?
GulfTalent's average is AED 6,000 a month, within a range of AED 3,500 to 10,500, based on 69 designers. A 2026 recruiter guide from Bespoke Careers puts total packages for established firms at AED 13,000 to 23,000 a month for intermediate designers and AED 20,000 to 40,000 for senior designers.
How do I become an interior designer after engineering?
Use your drawing and site skills, then add design principles, materials, lighting and rendering software. Build two or three interior projects for your portfolio, and apply for roles such as design coordinator or junior designer at fit-out and design-and-build firms.
Which software do interior designers use?
AutoCAD for drawings, SketchUp for 3D modelling, V-Ray or 3ds Max for renders, and Photoshop for presentation. Larger firms also use BIM software such as Revit.
What should an interior design portfolio include?
Four to six projects, each with the brief, a plan, concept and mood board, renders, working drawings and process sheets, plus a one-page PDF summary to attach to applications.
