Carpet area is the usable floor area inside the walls of a flat, the space you can actually use. Built-up area adds the walls and balcony, and super built-up area adds your share of the building's common areas. Here is the short version, followed by the legal definition and a worked floor plan where every figure is calculated, the way an architect would.

Built-up areaCarpet area plus external walls, shafts and your own balcony.Market term, not in RERA
Super built-up areaBuilt-up area plus a share of lobbies, lifts, stairs and amenities.Market term, not in RERA

TL;DR

  • Carpet area is the net usable floor area of a flat, as defined in Section 2(k) of India's RERA Act, 2016.
  • It includes internal partition walls but excludes external walls, shafts, and exclusive balconies and terraces.
  • Built-up area adds the external walls, shafts and balcony; its exact definition varies between builders.
  • Super built-up area adds your proportionate share of common areas such as lobbies, lifts and the clubhouse.
  • The loading factor is super built-up area divided by carpet area; 1.35 means you pay for 35% more area than you can use.
  • Dubai listings usually quote BUA, not carpet area, and what BUA includes varies, so always ask.

What Is Carpet Area?

Before RERA, "carpet area" meant different things to different builders. The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 fixed one legal definition:

RERA Act 2016, Section 2(k)
"carpet area" means the net usable floor area of an apartment, excluding the area covered by the external walls, areas under services shafts, exclusive balcony or verandah area and exclusive open terrace area, but includes the area covered by the internal partition walls of the apartment.

The Act's Explanation adds that an "exclusive" balcony, verandah or open terrace is one that is attached to the flat and meant for the exclusive use of the buyer. RERA also requires promoters to state the carpet area of the apartments when they register a project (Section 4(2)(h)), and Haryana RERA (Gurugram) regulations from 2021 go further: no apartment in a project under them may be sold on any basis other than carpet area.

Included in carpet area

  • All rooms: living, bedrooms, kitchen, toilets
  • Internal partition walls between rooms
  • Internal passages within the flat

Excluded from carpet area

  • External walls of the flat
  • Services shafts (plumbing, electrical ducts)
  • Exclusive balcony or verandah
  • Exclusive open terrace

ICICI Bank's home loan guide explains the loading factor with an example: a factor of 1.20 means the builder has added 20% to your carpet area, and it notes the factor is lower in projects with fewer amenities. The useful number for a buyer is the last one. If a builder quotes a price per sq ft of super built-up area, multiply it by the loading factor to get the effective price per sq ft of carpet area, the space you actually live in.

How Revit Area Plans Calculate It

Architects do not measure areas by hand on a real project. In Revit, areas come from area plans, which are separate from the room layout. Revit creates two area schemes by default: Gross Building (total constructed area, which cannot be edited) and Rentable (based on an office measurement standard). You can add your own, such as a "RERA Carpet" scheme.

1. Area scheme and planCreate a scheme, for example "RERA Carpet", then an area plan for each floor under it.
2. Area boundariesDraw boundary lines on the inside face of the external walls and around each shaft, and keep the balcony as a separate area so it can be excluded.
3. Area scheduleSchedule the areas by flat and type. The totals update whenever the walls move.
Watch out: adding up Revit room areas does not give RERA carpet area. Rooms are measured to the faces of the walls around them, so they leave out the internal partitions, which RERA includes. In the example above, the partitions take up about 3.3 m², so the room total would be about 76 m², not 79.4 m².

Area Measurement in the Gulf

If you compare an Indian flat with one in Dubai or Riyadh, the numbers are not like for like. Gulf listings do not use RERA carpet area or loading factors.

MarketWhat listings usually quoteWatch for
IndiaCarpet area (RERA), often alongside super built-up areaPrice per sq ft quoted on super built-up area
DubaiBUA (built-up area), usually in sq ft; GFA is used for planning approvalsSources disagree on what BUA includes: some include walls, balconies and terraces, others measure inside the walls
Saudi ArabiaArea in square metresLabels can be unclear; check whether the figure is the plot, the building or the unit

In Dubai, Engel & Völkers notes that BUA is typically the number used in property listings and usually exceeds GFA, while dubizzle describes BUA as the internal floor area and says covered balconies are included only in some cases. With two respected sources differing, the safe move is to ask the agent exactly what the stated area includes. In Saudi Arabia, one Riyadh listing we checked showed the same "2,400" as square metres in one place and square feet in another, so confirm the unit and the basis against the title deed before comparing prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is carpet area?
Carpet area is the usable floor area inside a flat's external walls. Under RERA 2016 it includes internal partition walls but excludes external walls, services shafts, and exclusive balconies, verandahs and open terraces.
What is the difference between carpet area and built-up area?
Built-up area is carpet area plus the area of the external walls, services shafts and the flat's own balcony. It is always larger than carpet area, and since RERA does not define it, builders calculate it in slightly different ways.
How is super built-up area calculated?
Super built-up area is built-up area plus the flat's proportionate share of common areas such as lobbies, lifts, staircases and amenities. It can also be written as carpet area multiplied by the loading factor.
What is carpet area as per RERA?
Section 2(k) of the RERA Act 2016 defines carpet area as the net usable floor area of an apartment, excluding the area covered by the external walls, areas under services shafts, exclusive balcony or verandah area and exclusive open terrace area, but including the area covered by the internal partition walls.
What is the loading factor?
The loading factor is the ratio of super built-up area to carpet area. A loading factor of 1.35 means the area you pay for is 35% larger than the area you can use. ICICI Bank's guide notes it is lower in projects with fewer amenities.
