Short answer

A fire fighting system detects, controls and puts out fires in a building using water, gas or chemical agents. It ranges from a fire extinguisher on a wall to a pumped network of hydrants, risers and sprinklers.

This page is the overview. Each main system has its own detailed guide, listed below. If you are an MEP or BIM engineer, this is the map of what the fire services contractor will put in your ceilings, shafts and plant rooms.

TL;DR

  • A fire fighting system detects, controls and extinguishes fires with water, gas or chemical agents.
  • Active protection fights the fire; passive protection contains it with fire-rated walls, doors and dampers.
  • Seven systems cover most buildings: hydrants, sprinklers, wet risers, dry risers, hose reels, extinguishers and gas suppression.
  • Water systems share one backbone: a fire water tank, fire pumps, a ring main, alarm valves and a fire brigade inlet.
  • India follows NBC Part 4 and IS standards; the UAE and Saudi Arabia follow their fire codes, which lean on NFPA standards.
  • In BIM, fire services are modelled in Revit as fire protection piping systems and coordinated with HVAC, plumbing and electrical.

What Is a Fire Fighting System?

A fire fighting system in a building is the set of equipment that does three jobs:

DetectSmoke, heat or flame detectors, and the sprinkler bulb itself, sense a fire.
ControlSprinklers and gas systems hold the fire back or put it out automatically.
ExtinguishFirefighters and trained occupants use hydrants, risers, hose reels and extinguishers.

On a project, this is the "F" in MEPF, and it is designed by a fire protection engineer and coordinated with the other MEP services.

Fire hydrant system

A pumped network of pressurised pipes feeding landing valves and yard hydrants, so firefighters can connect hoses anywhere around and inside the building. Indian codes call for at least 3.5 kg/cm² at the hydraulically most remote hydrant (IS 13039).

Full guide: Fire hydrant system

Sprinkler system

Pipes with heat-sensitive heads that open one by one over a fire. Comes in wet, dry, pre-action and deluge types, designed in India to IS 15105 and in the Gulf largely to NFPA 13.

Full guide: Fire sprinkler system

Wet riser

A vertical pipe kept full of water, with automatic pumps and stored water behind it, and a landing valve on each floor (BAFSA).

Full guide: Wet riser (coming soon)

Dry riser

An empty vertical pipe that the fire brigade fills from its tender through an inlet on the outside wall. In UK guidance, dry or wet risers may be used between 18 m and 50 m, and only wet risers above 50 m (BAFSA).

Hose reel

A permanently charged rubber hose on a drum next to the landing valve, for occupants to tackle a fire early. IS 3844 covers internal hydrants and hose reels.

Fire extinguishers

Portable first-aid equipment chosen by fire class: A (solids), B (flammable liquids), C (gases) and D (metals). IS 2190:2010 covers selection, installation and maintenance; for energised electrical equipment it allows only dry powder, CO₂ or clean agent types (IS 2190 text).

Gas suppression

A total flooding system that fills a closed room with a gas agent, used where water would cause damage, such as data processing, telecom and art storage. Clean agents include HFC-227ea (FM-200), FK-5-1-12 (Novec 1230) and inert gases IG-541 and IG-55, under NFPA 2001 (Consulting-Specifying Engineer).

Main Components: Tanks, Pumps, Pipes and Valves

Hydrants, risers and sprinklers usually share one water backbone:

Fire water tankDedicated storage, sized by occupancy and height under NBC Table 7; tall buildings add a terrace tank.
Fire pumpsMain electric pump, diesel standby pump and a small jockey pump that keeps the system pressurised.
Ring mainA looped main around the building, so water reaches any outlet from two directions.
Alarm valvesSprinkler control stations that raise the alarm when water starts to flow.
Fire brigade inletLets fire tenders pump water into the system from outside.

The pumps are the heart of the system: when pressure drops, the main pump starts, and the diesel pump takes over if power fails. They get their own detailed guide. Full guide: Fire pump (coming soon)

Fire Codes: NBC India, UAE and Saudi Arabia

India: NBC 2016 Part 4

  • Part 4, Fire and Life Safety; Table 7 sets the systems, tanks and pumps needed by occupancy and height
  • Design and equipment to IS standards: IS 13039 (hydrants), IS 15105 (sprinklers), IS 3844 (internal hydrants and hose reels), IS 2190 (extinguishers)
  • Approved by state fire services

UAE: Fire and Life Safety Code

  • UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice (2018 edition), enforced by each emirate's Civil Defence
  • Hydrants to NFPA 24, pumps to NFPA 20, sprinklers widely to NFPA 13
  • Abu Dhabi issued 54 local amendments on 12 March 2026

Saudi Arabia: SBC 801

  • Saudi Fire Code (2018), based on the 2015 International Fire Code with NFPA standards
  • Section 903 sprinklers, 905 standpipes, 912 fire department connections, 913 fire pumps; Section 507 and Appendix C for water supplies and hydrants
  • Standpipes to NFPA 14

India. NBC 2016 Part 4 Table 7 sets the minimum fire fighting installations for each occupancy and height (Table 7, Kerala Fire and Rescue Services copy), and Delhi Fire Service requires sprinkler design to IS 15105 with plans and calculations submitted with the building plans (Delhi Fire Service).

NBCS 2026. BIS has published the National Building Construction Standards 2026 (SP 7:2026), reported as effective from 30 April 2026 and replacing NBC 2016. Reports say fire and life safety norms now apply to residential buildings from 24 m (previously 15 m), with "should" replacing "shall" in the fire section (Drishti IAS; ULBA). Check which edition and state bye-laws apply.

UAE. The UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice governs fire systems, enforced by Civil Defence in each emirate. Hydrant systems follow the code with NFPA 24 and pumps follow NFPA 20 (MMJ), and NFPA 13 is widely adopted for sprinklers (BITO). Abu Dhabi Civil Defence released 54 amendments on 12 March 2026 covering items including fire pumps and hydrants (Vortex Fire).

Saudi Arabia. SBC 801 is based on the 2015 International Fire Code and NFPA standards, revised in 2018 (Pillai, ASRJETS 2025). Its fire protection chapter covers sprinklers (903), standpipes (905), fire department connections (912) and fire pumps (913) (SBC 801 contents).

How Fire Fighting Systems Are Modelled in Revit

In a BIM project, fire services are modelled in Revit alongside the other MEP systems:

  • Fire protection piping systems: Revit has system classifications for wet, dry, pre-action and other (user-defined) fire protection systems (Revit API reference).
  • Sprinkler and equipment families: place pendent, upright and sidewall sprinkler families, landing valves, hose reels, pumps and valves, then connect the piping.
  • Clash coordination: run Collaborate › Coordinate › Interference Check between fire pipes and ducts, cable trays and structure; the report lists every conflicting pair (Autodesk Help).
  • Penetrations: mark where pipes cross fire-rated walls, so fire stopping is planned, not improvised on site.

Hydraulic calculations for sprinklers and hydrants are usually done in dedicated fire protection software, with Revit carrying the coordinated model, drawings and quantities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the types of fire fighting systems?
The seven main types in buildings are fire hydrant systems, sprinkler systems, wet risers, dry risers, hose reels, fire extinguishers and gas suppression systems.
What is the difference between active and passive fire protection?
Active fire protection reacts to a fire to fight it: sprinklers, hydrants, extinguishers, detectors and alarms. Passive fire protection does not act; it contains the fire with fire-rated walls, floors, doors, dampers and fire stopping.
Which code governs fire fighting systems in India?
The National Building Code of India, Part 4 (Fire and Life Safety), with IS standards such as IS 13039, IS 15105, IS 3844 and IS 2190, enforced through state fire services. BIS has now published NBCS 2026 (SP 7:2026) as its successor, so check which edition your authority applies.
What is a fire fighting system in a building?
It is the equipment that detects, controls and puts out fires in the building: water-based systems such as hydrants, risers, hose reels and sprinklers, plus extinguishers and gas suppression, all fed from a fire water tank and fire pumps.
Which software is used to design fire fighting systems?
Revit MEP (and AutoCAD) for modelling, drawings and coordination, with dedicated hydraulic calculation software for sprinkler and hydrant sizing. Navisworks or Revit's Interference Check is used for clash detection.
