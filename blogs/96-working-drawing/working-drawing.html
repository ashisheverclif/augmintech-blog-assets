Working drawings are the full set of dimensioned, annotated drawings that a building is actually built from: site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules, each on a numbered sheet. Below, we walk through a real sample set, sheet by sheet, in the order it is issued.

If you are a student or junior architect preparing your first drawing set, every sheet below is one you will draw, and the checklists tell you what an engineer or contractor expects to find on it.

TL;DR

  • A working drawing set is the dimensioned, buildable version of the design, not the presentation drawings used to win the client.
  • Sets are issued in a fixed order: cover and sheet list, site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, details, then schedules.
  • India's IS 962:1989 sets the basics: dimensions in millimetres, A-series sheets, a title block at bottom right, and thick lines at least twice as thick as thin ones.
  • Sheet numbers like A-101 read as discipline, sheet type and sequence, so anyone can find the plans or the details at a glance.
  • The construction set combines architectural, structural and MEP drawings, each with its own letter prefix.
  • Revit builds the whole set from one model, so a change to a wall updates every plan, section and schedule it appears in.

What Is a Working Drawing?

A working drawing is a drawing with enough information to build from: exact dimensions, levels, materials, construction notes and references to other sheets. Together, working drawings form the drawing set that goes to the contractor, the structural and MEP engineers and, often, the approving authority.

They are different from the drawings that come before them. Concept drawings are sketches that explore ideas. Presentation drawings are rendered plans, elevations and 3D views made to explain the design to a client or jury, and they leave out dimensions and construction detail. Working drawings are plain, precise and coordinated, and they are the ones that carry legal and contractual weight on site.

Architectural vs Construction Drawings

Architectural drawings are one part of the construction set. The full set adds the engineers' drawings, each in its own numbered series, and the contractor builds from all of them read together.

SetPrefixPrepared byMain content
ArchitecturalAArchitectPlans, elevations, sections, details, door, window and finish schedules
StructuralSStructural engineerFoundations, column and beam layouts, slab reinforcement, bar bending schedules
Mechanical (HVAC)MMEP engineerDuct and pipe layouts, equipment schedules
PlumbingPMEP engineerWater supply, drainage, sanitary fixtures

Prefix letters follow the US National CAD Standard as explained by Archtoolbox. IS 962:1989 also requires drawing numbers in each series (architectural, structural, plumbing, electrical, mechanical) to carry a letter prefix.

In practice, the construction set is issued in stages, often called Issued for Construction (IFC), Good for Construction (GFC) or Approved for Construction (AFC), depending on the office. Each issue carries a revision letter or number in the title block so the site always works from the latest sheet.

Sheet Numbering and Drawing Standards

A sheet number tells you what is on the sheet before you open it. The most widely used format, from the US National CAD Standard (NCS), has three parts:

A
Discipline: A architectural, S structural, M mechanical
-
1
Sheet type: 1 means plans
01
Sequence: first sheet of that type, 01 to 99
0General
1Plans
2Elevations
3Sections
4Large-scale views
5Details
6Schedules, diagrams
7–8User-defined
93D views

Sequence numbers do not have to be consecutive, so you can leave gaps for sheets added later. Many Indian and Gulf offices use their own variation, such as A-01, A-02, but the logic of discipline plus number is the same.

In India, the reference standard for architectural drawings is IS 962:1989, Code of Practice for Architectural and Building Drawings (second revision). Its key rules:

Sheets and title block

  • A-series sheets, A0 to A4, using the smallest size that keeps the drawing clear
  • Title block at bottom right, visible when the print is folded
  • A revision panel recording number, description, date and approval

Dimensions and scales

  • Dimensions normally in millimetres (cl. 10.2.6)
  • Unit can be omitted if a note states it for the whole drawing
  • Scales chosen per IS 10713, from the recommended list

Lines and lettering

  • Two line thicknesses, thick at least twice the thin
  • Thick for outlines, thin for dimensions and hatching
  • Prints folded to A4 with the title block on top

How Revit Produces a Drawing Set from One Model

In AutoCAD, each plan, section and elevation is a separate drawing that you keep in step by hand. In Revit, they are all views of one 3D model. Move a wall and every plan, section, elevation and schedule that shows it updates.

ModelWalls, floors, doors, windows
›
ViewsPlans, sections, elevations, details
›
SheetsTitle block plus placed views
›
SchedulesDoors, windows, sheet list
  1. Create sheets. Go to View › Sheet Composition › Sheet, pick a title block, then fill in the sheet number and name.
  2. Place views. Drag plans, sections and elevations from the Project Browser onto the sheet. A view can sit on only one sheet; duplicate it to show it on another.
  3. Add schedules. Door and window schedules read their tags, sizes and counts straight from the model.
  4. Build the sheet list. View › Schedules › Sheet List creates a schedule of sheets that Autodesk describes as a table of contents for the set.
  5. Issue revisions. Record each issue in the project's revisions, and the revision schedule in each affected sheet's title block lists it.

Submission Drawings for the Gulf

Gulf authorities set their own drawing submission formats and portals, and these change, so always download the current checklist for your project. In Dubai, applications for a new building permit go through the Dubai Building Permits System (BPS), and a July 2024 guide lists these drawing packages:

Architectural drawings
Structural drawings
Electromechanical (MEP) drawings
Thermal insulation and drainage plans
Road layouts
Civil Defense plans

The application is reviewed by several government authorities through the BPS, and Dubai Municipality then issues the permit and stamps the drawings.

BIM models too: since 1 January 2024, Dubai requires BIM model submissions for building permits on larger projects, including buildings over 20 floors or 20,000 m² for architectural designs, specialised buildings such as hospitals and universities, and government projects, with models submitted in IFC format (as summarised on Autodesk's Dubai BIM mandate page). Check the Dubai Municipality circular for the current thresholds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a working drawing in architecture?
A working drawing is a dimensioned, annotated drawing with enough information to build from. The full working drawing set includes the site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules.
What drawings are included in a working drawing set?
In issue order: a cover sheet with the sheet list and general notes, the site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, details, and door, window and finish schedules. The full construction set adds structural and MEP drawings.
What is the difference between working drawings and construction drawings?
Working drawings usually means the architect's buildable drawings. Construction drawings is the wider set issued for building, which combines the architectural, structural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical and fire drawings into one coordinated package.
What scale are working drawings drawn at?
Under IS 962:1989, site plans are typically 1:500 or 1:1000, building plans 1:50 or 1:100, and details 1:10 or 1:20. Elevations and sections usually match the plan scale. Dimensions are written in millimetres.
Can Revit create working drawings?
Yes. Revit generates plans, sections, elevations and schedules as views of one model, places them on sheets with title blocks, and keeps them all updated when the design changes. It can also produce a sheet list for the whole set.
