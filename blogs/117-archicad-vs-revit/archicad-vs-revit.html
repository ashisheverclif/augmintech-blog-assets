Archicad and Revit are both full BIM programs that model buildings and produce coordinated drawing sets. The main differences: Archicad runs natively on Mac and Windows, has its own architecture-focused workflows; Revit runs only on Windows but covers architecture, structure and MEP in one platform and has a large add-in ecosystem. Both offer education access for eligible students, subject to their terms. The right choice depends less on features than on what your target employers and consultants use. This guide compares workflows, collaboration, cost, hardware and learning requirements in detail.

TL;DR

  • Both are complete BIM tools for modelling, documentation, schedules and team collaboration.
  • Archicad runs on Mac and Windows; Revit runs only on Windows.
  • Compare current regional quotations for the authoring, teamwork and analysis capabilities you need. A lower headline price may exclude required services.
  • Revit is stronger for multi-discipline projects where structure and MEP teams also work in Autodesk tools.
  • Both are free for students: Archicad's EDU licence and Autodesk's education plan.
  • Choose by your market: read job adverts in the city where you want to work.

Modelling Workflow

Both programs model buildings with intelligent elements (walls, slabs, roofs, doors, windows, stairs) and generate plans, sections and elevations from the model. The way they organise that model differs:

Archicad's approach

  • The building is organised by stories
  • Elements are placed with tools and settings dialogs; complex shapes use tools such as Morph
  • Libraries of parametric objects for doors, windows and furniture
  • A drafting-friendly feel that many former 2D users find natural

Revit's approach

  • The building is organised by levels and grids
  • Elements belong to families and types with parameters
  • Strong parametric relationships: walls attach to levels and roofs, doors host in walls
  • Custom families built in the Family Editor

In practice, both handle typical architecture well. Revit's family system is powerful but takes time to master; Archicad's object approach is often described as quicker to get productive in. For unusual forms, both connect to other tools, and both export IFC for exchange.

Team Collaboration

Archicad Teamwork on BIMcloudTeam members reserve the elements they work on and send and receive changes through BIMcloud. Graphisoft says its Delta Server technology keeps network traffic to a minimum, so teams can work together over ordinary internet connections, and users can keep working on reserved elements if the connection drops. The Studio plan includes in-house teamwork; Collaborate adds Graphisoft-hosted cloud collaboration.
Revit worksharingThe team shares a central model, each person working in a local copy. You edit elements in worksets you own or borrow, and synchronise with central to publish your changes and receive others'. Autodesk recommends synchronising at least daily and creating a fresh local copy every day. Cloud collaboration is available through Autodesk's cloud services.

For multi-company projects, both rely on exchanging models. Revit teams often coordinate with structural and MEP consultants who also use Revit, and run clash checks in Navisworks Manage, which Autodesk describes as identifying cross-discipline interferences earlier in design. Archicad teams typically coordinate through IFC with consultants using other software.

IFC and Working With Other Software

IFC, the open BIM exchange format, lets Archicad and Revit models be combined and reviewed together. That matters where authorities ask for it: since 1 January 2024, Dubai has required BIM model submissions in IFC format for building permits on larger buildings, according to Autodesk's summary of the mandate. Both programs export IFC; in either, the quality of the export depends on how carefully the model is classified and checked, so learning IFC export settings is worth the time whichever you choose.

Four Myths About Archicad and Revit

  1. "Archicad is only for small projects." It runs large projects with Teamwork on BIMcloud; the deciding factor is usually the team's ecosystem, not size.
  2. "Revit is only for big firms." Solo architects use it too, though Revit LT lacks several features teams rely on.
  3. "You must pick one forever." Concepts transfer, and many architects learn both over a career.
  4. "IFC makes the choice irrelevant." IFC helps teams exchange models, but each office still works daily in one native tool, so match your employers.

Get Familiar With the Actual Interfaces

The screenshots above show genuine application interfaces. Their captions identify the recorded versions so you can distinguish these examples from your installed release. Use the named controls to understand how each program connects model navigation with drawing production.

In Archicad, distinguish a model viewpoint from a saved view with documentation settings. In Revit, distinguish the element properties from the properties of the active view. In either tool, a model can be correct while its drawing looks wrong because of the saved view settings.

Compare Them With the Same Revision Test

Use a small apartment exercise. Produce a plan, an internal elevation, a section, a door schedule and one issued sheet in each tool. Keep the brief and geometry identical; do not compare an experienced user's template against a beginner's empty file.

  1. Model: set stories or levels, place walls and hosted openings, and classify objects appropriately.
  2. Document: establish view settings, annotate, schedule and assemble the sheet.
  3. Revise: move one door 300 mm and change one finish specification.
  4. Check: inspect all affected views, quantities, manual notes and the exported PDF.
  5. Reflect: record setup effort, repeated editing and errors caught, rather than claiming one tool is always faster.

Check an IFC exchange, not just a successful export

Agree the exchange purpose, IFC version, coordinates, units and required properties before exporting. Open the result in the receiving workflow and compare a known dimension, floor elevations, element categories, opening positions and a small sample of properties. Record missing items and export settings. IFC exchange does not promise a fully editable native replica.

Common first-project problems

A missing object may be a visibility or renovation/phase issue, not deleted geometry. A wrong quantity may come from schedule criteria or categories. A displaced consultant model may indicate coordinate disagreement. Diagnose the settings first; duplicating objects or moving a linked model by eye can hide the problem.

For a course decision, ask to see the final model, documentation and review process. BIM skills become transferable through clean information and checked outputs, but learning a new interface still takes practice. No fixed switching time applies to every student.

Switching From One to the Other

  • What transfers: BIM thinking, drawing standards, construction knowledge, schedules and coordination habits.
  • What you relearn: the interface, how elements are organised (stories vs levels, objects vs families), and graphic controls.
  • Files: move projects through IFC; expect to rebuild complex objects and check every element after import.
  • Versions: Revit files open only in the same or a newer release, so agree one version for every team member.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Archicad better than Revit?
Neither is better overall. Archicad suits Mac users and architecture-focused practices ; Revit suits multi-discipline teams and has a larger add-in ecosystem. Choose what your market uses.
Is Archicad easier to learn than Revit?
Many users find Archicad quicker to get productive in, especially coming from 2D drafting. Revit's families and view controls take longer to master. Both need solid architectural knowledge.
Can I use Revit on a Mac?
Not natively; Revit is Windows-only. Mac users run Windows in a virtual machine. Archicad runs natively on macOS.
How much do Archicad and Revit cost?
Prices depend on region, plan, billing term and collaboration entitlements. Check current vendor quotations; eligible students can apply for education access under the respective terms.
Can Archicad and Revit work together?
Yes, through IFC. Teams often combine an Archicad architectural model with Revit structural or MEP models for coordination, checking each import carefully.
Which is used more in India and the Gulf?
It varies by firm and city, and reliable market-share data is not public. Check job adverts in your target city; many list Revit, some list Archicad, and some accept either.
Does Archicad have MEP?
Yes, MEP modelling is included in the Archicad Collaborate plan; Studio users can trial it but need Collaborate for real work.
